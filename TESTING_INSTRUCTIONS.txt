Testing Instructions - GoIndia Excel Add-in

Overview
Authenticates users and populates Excel with company financial data. Two optional features call a third-party AI service (OpenRouter): an AI Financial Model builder, and DataGPT, a chat assistant that answers questions and can write to the sheet. Only company financial figures are sent - never credentials or personal data.

Setup
1. Excel > Insert > Get Add-ins > My Add-ins > Upload My Add-in > upload manifest.xml.
2. A "GoIndia Stocks" tab appears in the ribbon (not the Home tab). It has four buttons: Download Data, Ask DataGPT, Feedback, Profile.
3. Click any button, then sign in:
   Username: ut@gmail.com
   Password: 123

Test account note: this account is not a real wallet customer, so its balance always reads Rs 0.00. It is deliberately exempt from all balance checks - the AI Financial Model toggle and the DataGPT send button both stay enabled despite the Rs 0.00.

A. Download Data
4. Click Download Data. Select a company (e.g. "ACC Ltd."). Leave the data-format tickboxes at their defaults.
5. Optional: turn ON the AI Financial Model card (OFF by default). This sends the downloaded financials to OpenRouter to build a 5-year projected model. Takes ~30-90+ seconds with a progress status.
6. Click Download Data. Sheets Key Financials, Quarterly Data, Annual Data and Index populate. If the AI model was enabled, 7 more sheets prefixed "FM " appear (Assumptions, Operational, P&L, Balance Sheet, Capex & FCF, Valuation & DCF, Summary).
7. Verify all sheets populate correctly and FM sheets are fully computed, not blank.

B. DataGPT
8. Click Ask DataGPT. Type a question, e.g. "What was ACC's revenue last year?" and press Enter.
9. Progress steps appear while it works, then a formatted answer. Responses take ~10-60 seconds.
10. The mode picker above the message box toggles between "Ask before writing" (default - prompts before editing the sheet) and "Write automatically". Test either.

C. Feedback and Profile
11. Click Feedback, enter any text, submit. A confirmation appears.
12. Click Profile to view the username, wallet balance with recent charges, and the Disclaimer. Confirm the Privacy Policy link in the manifest opens correctly.

Expected Results
- Add-in loads without errors; login via credentials or Google works.
- Company selection fetches correct data; FM sheets compute fully if enabled.
- DataGPT returns a relevant answer and respects the selected write mode.
- Feedback submits; Profile shows Rs 0.00 with AI Financial Model and DataGPT both still usable.

Notes
- Test in Excel desktop or Excel on the web. All backend calls use HTTPS.
- Test account is pre-configured to simulate customer use.
