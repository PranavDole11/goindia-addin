Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-09-08

This privacy policy explains how the GoIndia Stocks Excel Add-in (“the Add-in”) collects, uses, and protects information.

Information We Collect

When you use this Excel add-in, we may collect the following information:

  • Your login credentials used within the GoIndia Stocks Excel add-in (including via Google login).
  • Selection of the company from the dropdown menu.
  • Data retrieved from our backend servers specific to the selected company.

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Authenticate you securely to access your account.
  • Populate Excel with the data corresponding to the company you select.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal login credentials with third parties. Company-specific data remains within our systems and is accessed only by authorized personnel for operational purposes, except as described below for optional AI-assisted features.

Optional AI-Assisted Features

The Add-in includes two optional, off-by-default features — the "AI Financial Model" builder and the "Ask DataGPT" chat assistant — that use a third-party AI service (OpenRouter, which routes requests to underlying models such as DeepSeek, Google Gemini, or OpenAI) to generate financial models, answer questions, and draft spreadsheet content.

  • If you enable the AI Financial Model, the company's already-downloaded financial and operational data is sent to OpenRouter to generate projected model figures.
  • If you use Ask DataGPT, your typed questions, company financial/research data it retrieves on your behalf, and any workbook cell contents you ask it to read are sent to OpenRouter to generate its response.

No login credentials or personal account information are included in either request — only company-level financial/research data, your typed question, and workbook content you explicitly ask the assistant to read. Both features are off by default and only send data when you actively use them.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and secure storage on our servers.
